Pocket Arcade

A bookmark that opens every Game Bookshelf game on top of any page you're on. New games show up in it automatically. Click it again to hide the game, then once more to bring it back right where you left off.

🎮 GB Arcade
  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on Mac).
  2. Drag the GB Arcade button onto the bar.
  3. On any page, click the bookmark and pick a game. Use ☰ All games to switch.

Can't drag? Click Copy code, make a new bookmark, and paste it into the URL field.

A few sites block embedded pages. If a game stays blank, press ↗ in the arcade bar to open it in a new tab.
