How to play

Shooting

Mouseaim the cue at where you point
Hold left button, pull backset power, let go to shoot
Mouse wheel, A D, ← →fine aim (hold Shift for tiny steps)
Spacehold to charge power, let go to shoot
↑ ↓ Q Efollow / draw and left / right spin

Esc or Ppause

On a phone: drag on the table to aim, use the arrows for fine aim, pull the power bar down and let go. Tap the spin ball to put english on the shot.

8-ball

The table is open after the break. The first ball you sink after the break makes that group yours: solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15). Clear your group, then sink the 8 to win. Sink the 8 early and you lose the frame.

9-ball

Always hit the lowest numbered ball first. Anything that drops on a legal shot counts, so sinking the 9 at any time on a legal shot wins.

Fouls

Scratching (the cue ball drops), hitting the wrong ball first, or hitting nothing into a cushion after contact gives your opponent ball in hand: they can place the cue ball anywhere.
