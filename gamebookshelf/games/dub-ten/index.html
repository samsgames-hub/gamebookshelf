How to ride

On the snow

A D carve. W tucks for speed. S brakes. Bigger kickers need more speed, so watch the number in the corner.

Hold Space to load a pop and let go right at the lip to go higher. Press A or D while loading to wind up a spin.

Hold B to butter on your tips, or B with S on your tails. Steer to spin on the snow, let go to bounce out.

In the air

A D spin. S backflips, W frontflips. The arrow keys roll you sideways, ↑ throws a rodeo.

A spin and a backflip started together at the lip make a cork. Start the flip first and the spin only tilts it.

Shift mute, Q tail and E japan grabs. Hold a grab for a quarter second for it to count. Different grabs in one air stack up.

Rails and boxes

Land on a rail or ride onto a box. Hold a direction as you get on to turn your skis across it. Tap A or D to swap, up to twice per rail, and let go of Space to pop off. Spinning on and off counts.

Scoring

Spins and flips score more the bigger they get: a 720 is worth four times a 360. Riding switch adds a quarter, off-axis tricks add almost a third, and a clean landing adds a bonus.

Chain tricks into a line. Each extra trick raises the line multiplier. Ride out a clean landing for three seconds to bank it. Crash and the whole line is gone.

Repeating the same trick in your last three airs pays less. Spin the other way or ride switch to get full points back.

Hold R to restart, C changes the camera, and you can hold the right mouse button to look around. Gamepads work too.
