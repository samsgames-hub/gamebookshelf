Know your enemy

Infantry fall fast to machine guns and snipers. Medics heal soldiers around them.

Jeeps, APCs and tanks shrug off bullets. Anti-tank guns and mortars crack armor. APCs drop a squad when destroyed.

Helicopters and drones fly straight at the base and ignore the road. Only flak, and a little machine gun and sniper fire, can reach them.

Defenses get promoted as they rack up kills and hit harder with every rank.

Upgrades, abilities and merging

Every defense has three upgrade paths, firepower, rate of fire and range, with four tiers each. Buy only the ones you need.

Each defense has its own ability with a cooldown, like suppressing fire, sabot rounds, napalm or a radar rally. A pulsing amber marker means it's ready.

Two defenses of the same type at tier 6 or higher can merge into one elite defense with much stronger stats and a faster, stronger ability.

Flame bunkers roast infantry and light vehicles up close. Radar posts don't shoot; they boost the damage and fire rate of every defense in their coverage.
