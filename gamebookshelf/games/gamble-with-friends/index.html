Gamble With Friends

Team up, play any table you like, and hit the chip goal before the clock runs out.

How to play
  • Create a room and share the invite link, or join with a friend’s 4-letter code.
  • Everyone starts with 1,000 chips and plays any table. All your chips count toward one team total.
  • Reach the stage goal before the clock runs out. Goals grow every stage.
  • Clearing a stage pays gold. Spend it in the shop on power-ups, like the Totem that reverses your last loss.
  • If the clock wins, the run is over. How far can your team get?
