      How to play

      Call a play

      Pick from 8 plays. On pass plays you’re the quarterback. On HB Dive and HB Toss you’re the running back. Glowing lines show each route.

      Snap

      Click the screen to grab the mouse, then press Space to snap. Move with WASD and look with the mouse.

      Throw

      Hold left click to power up and release to throw where you’re looking. Look higher to throw deeper. The ring shows where it lands, and markers show who’s open: green open, yellow tight, red covered.

      Run

      After a catch you control the receiver. Shift sprints, Space jukes (hold A or D to pick a side) and E or right-click stiff-arms whoever’s in front of you.

      Downs

      Gain 10 yards (the yellow line) in four tries for a first down. On 3rd and 4th down you can kick a field goal: aim, hold left click and release at full power. On 4th down you can punt.

      Watch out

      Hold the ball too long and you’ll get sacked. Throw into coverage and it gets picked off. If you cross the blue line you can’t throw anymore.
