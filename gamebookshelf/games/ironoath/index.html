The Rules of the Lists

  • A foe who turtles behind his guard can be kicked open. Hold your own guard too long and he will do the same to you.
  • Stamina (green) pays for every swing, block and dodge. Run dry and your guard breaks.
  • A gold flash and a chime on the enemy weapon mean: press block now. A parry staggers your foe. Your next hit is a riposte for extra damage.
  • A foe whose blade flashes white is winding up a heavy. Parry it or step away.
  • A red glow means an Oath Strike. It cannot be blocked — dodge.
  • Wounds carry over. Spend your winnings at the armoury between duels.
  • Every fifth duel is a Champion. Fell the third to win the trial.
