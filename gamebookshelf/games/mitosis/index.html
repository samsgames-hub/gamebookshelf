    How to play

    Move

    Your cells follow the mouse. On a phone, drag anywhere. Bigger cells are slower.

    Eat

    Glowing pellets give mass. You can swallow any cell about a quarter smaller than you. Anything bigger can swallow you.

    Split · Space

    Each cell splits in half and launches its half at the mouse. Great for catching runners, but each half is easier to eat. Halves rejoin after a while.

    Eject · hold W

    Shoot out blobs of mass. Feed a teammate, bait a hunter, or feed a virus 7 times to fire a new virus at someone.

    Viruses

    The spiky green cells. Small cells can hide under them. Anything over 133 mass that touches one bursts into pieces.

    Nutrient blooms

    Every minute or so a golden bloom of rich pellets appears, marked on your map. Everyone races for it.
