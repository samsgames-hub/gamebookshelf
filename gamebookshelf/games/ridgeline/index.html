On a phone, hold the Gas and Brake pedals at the bottom of the screen.

Driving

Drive as far as you can before the fuel runs out. Grab red jerrycans to fill up. If the cab hits the ground, the run is over.

Gas pushes the nose up and brake pulls it down, on the ground and in the air. Ease off near the top of steep climbs so you don't tip over backwards.

Low range doubles the pulling power and caps your speed. Use it to crawl up rock ledges, then switch back.

Air time, flips, supply caches and base camps pay extra cash. Spend it in the garage.
