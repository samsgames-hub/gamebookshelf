Skyline

Build a skyscraper, one floor at a time.

How to play

  • Tap, click or press Space to drop the sliding floor.
  • Whatever hangs over the edge is cut off and falls. The tower gets narrower.
  • Land it dead on for a Perfect. Three Perfects in a row and floors start growing back.
  • Gold floors show up every so often. Land one cleanly for a pile of coins.
  • Above floor 25 the wind picks up and floors speed up and slow down.
  • Spend coins on new building styles. Finish missions for bonus coins.
  • Esc or P pauses. R restarts.
