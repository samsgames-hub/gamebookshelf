Slingshot

Sling a probe from planet to planet, out to the edge of the system.

How to play

  • Your probe orbits a planet. Tap, click or press Space to let go. It flies off in a straight line, along the direction it was moving.
  • Fly into the next planet's dotted orbit to be caught. Hit the planet itself and you crash.
  • Tap again while flying to fire a course correction: a short burn that bends you toward the nearest planet ahead. You only get a few, shown at the top left. You get one back in each new sector.
  • Jump straight past a planet to the one after it for a Skip bonus.
  • Grab stars on the way and spend them in the Hangar on upgrades and new probes.
  • Further out: asteroids, planets that drift, and black holes that pull you in.
  • Esc or P pauses. R restarts.
