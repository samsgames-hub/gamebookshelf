HOW TO PLAY

1 · DROP

A cargo plane crosses the island. Press Space or click to jump, then steer your glider (WASD) to a town. Busy towns mean better loot and earlier fights.

2 · LOOT

Grab weapons, ammo and heals with E. Golden chests hum when you're near and drop better gear. Rarity runs Common › Uncommon › Rare › Epic › Legendary.

3 · FIGHT

Mouse aims, Left-click fires, Right-click aims down sights for accuracy (snipers scope out). R reloads, 1–5 or wheel swaps slots. Walls block your vision. Bushes hide you until you shoot.

4 · SURVIVE

The storm closes in phases. Check the timer and the white circle on your map (M). Select a heal and hold fire to use it. Shields go on top of health. Supply drops carry Epic and Legendary gear.

Touch: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires. Tap the hotbar to switch slots.
