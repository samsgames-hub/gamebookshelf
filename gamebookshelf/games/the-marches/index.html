How to Play

Controls

WASDMove (hold Shift to sprint)
MouseLook around (arrow keys also turn the camera)
Left click / JStrike — click again to chain three cuts
FHeavy blow
Right click / QBlock. Press on the gold flash to parry
SpaceRoll — you cannot be hit mid-roll
EOpen chests, gather herbs, pray, rest
RUse a bandage
Tab / M / EscPack / map / pause

On a phone: left stick to move, drag the right side to look, and use the buttons.

The Marches

  • Three bandit camps (red squares on the map) hold the land. Clear all three and the gate of the Black Keep opens.
  • Chests hide better weapons and armour. The further from Greywatch, the better the find.
  • A gold flash on a weapon means block now to parry. A red glow cannot be blocked — roll away.
  • Rest at a campfire to heal, save and buy supplies. Resting also wakes the wilds again.
  • Standing stones grant a lasting blessing. Three herbs make a bandage.
  • Fall, and you wake at your last fire with a quarter of your gold gone.
