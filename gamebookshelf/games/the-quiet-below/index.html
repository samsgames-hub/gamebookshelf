HOW TO PLAY

THE RULE

The infected are nearly blind. They hunt by sound. Every step, shot and door makes noise — watch the NOISE meter. Sprinting carries far. Sneaking is almost silent.

Holding your flashlight on one for a second wakes it. Sweep, don't stare — or switch it off.

A still one dies to a single axe blow. A flare pulls a room toward it so you can walk the other way. Guns solve problems and create new ones.

Read the logs. Find enough and the ending changes.
