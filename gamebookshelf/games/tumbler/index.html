Tumbler

A new safe every day. Five dials, six tries.

How to play

Open the safe in six tries. The combination is five dials. Each dial is set to one of ten things in a fixed order, and nothing is used twice. Today the order is :

After each try, every dial lights up and points you the right way:

  • Green: that dial is right.
  • Amber: one step away. The arrow says which way: ▲ or ▼ .
  • Dark: two or more steps away. The arrow still points the way.

Example (digits): a dial is really 6. You try 2: dark ▲, so it's 4 or higher. You try 7: amber ▼, so it's one lower. It's 6.

The keypad greys out anything your clues have already ruled out for the dial you're setting. On a keyboard: 0–9 sets a dial, ← → move between dials, ↑ ↓ step up or down, Enter tries it, Backspace clears.

Everyone gets the same safe each day. Open it to collect a vault payout: bonus coins in Skyline or stars in Slingshot. Practice safes are unlimited.
