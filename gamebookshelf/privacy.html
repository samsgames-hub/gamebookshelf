Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Overview

Game Bookshelf (gamebookshelf.com and its subdomains) is a free website with games you play in your browser. You don't need an account to play; accounts are optional and only used to sync your saves. This page explains what information the site and its partners collect, and the choices you have.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ad Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some other companies' personalized ads at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count page views. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across other websites. It records things like which page was visited, the referring site, browser type, screen size and country, so we can see which games people play.

Game saves and settings

High scores, unlocks, trophies and settings (like volume) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them. Each subdomain (for example 3.gamebookshelf.com) keeps its own separate saves, unless you sign in to an account.

Accounts

Accounts are optional. If you create one, we store your username, your password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it securely and never shows it to us) and a copy of your game saves, trophies and settings, so they're the same on every device and every gamebookshelf.com address. This data is stored in Google Firebase and is only used to sync your progress. Signing in sets one cookie on gamebookshelf.com that keeps you signed in on its subdomains. We don't ask for your email or real name, so please don't use your real name as your username. You can delete your account and its online copy of your saves at any time from the Account page.

Online multiplayer

Some games have online rooms. When you play online, the name you choose, your room code and in-game actions (such as positions, scores and chips) are sent through Google Firebase or a real-time relay so other players in your room can see them. Don't use your real full name or personal details as your player name. Room data is only used to run the game.

Help form

The help form in the About section uses Google Forms. Anything you type into it is sent to us through Google so we can fix bugs and answer questions. Only include an email address if you want a reply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and some addresses run through Cloudflare. Like any web host, these services may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Children

Game Bookshelf is meant for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information through the help form, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions about privacy? Send them through the help form in the About section of the home page.
