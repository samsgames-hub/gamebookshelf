How it works. Each tournament uses the same course for everyone, all week: the same tower twists in Skyline, the same planets in Slingshot, the same arena and bots in Mitosis, the same trail and stock vehicle in Ridgeline, and the same rack and house cue in Corner Pocket. 🏆 marks past champions and 🏅 past podium finishers. Scores that are impossible for the time played are left off the boards. When the week ends (Monday 00:00 UTC, Sunday evening in the US), everyone who entered gets a prize. It's paid straight into your games next time you visit this page: Skyline coins, Slingshot stars, Gamble With Friends gold and vault keys.
