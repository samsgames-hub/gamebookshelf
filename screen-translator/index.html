Translate anything on your screen

Click Share screen and pick a screen, window or tab. The app reads the text with OCR and paints the translation right on top of it.

  1. Drag on the picture to translate only one area.
  2. Turn on Live to re-translate whenever the screen changes.
  3. Float window keeps the translations on top of your other apps.

Space translate · A live · O hold to see original · Esc clear area · F float
